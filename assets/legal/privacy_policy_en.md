# AI Explorer — Privacy Policy

**Yasvar Labs** · Draft for legal review · Effective date: _to be set at release_

AI Explorer is a learning app for children aged 5–7. We built it to collect as
little as possible. This policy explains exactly what the app does with data.

## What we do not collect

- **No voice recordings.** When your child answers Aiko out loud, speech is
  recognised **on the device**. Audio and the words recognised are never
  stored and never sent anywhere. If the device has no on-device speech
  model, the app skips the speaking step rather than using an online service.
- **No names or contact details.** The app never asks your child for a name,
  photo, email, phone number or location.
- **No ads, no analytics, no tracking.** The app contains no advertising,
  analytics or crash-reporting software, and no social or chat features.
- **No generative AI.** Aiko's lines are pre-recorded; nothing your child says
  is sent to an AI service.

## What stays on the device

The app stores, only on the phone or tablet:

- the chosen language and episode progress (which episodes and badges are done);
- parent settings, including a one-way hash of the parent PIN (never the PIN itself);
- a count, with times, of "tell a grown-up" safety prompts shown, so a parent can
  see that one happened. What the child said is never stored.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all of this.

## Purchases (parents only)

Premium is bought by a parent in the PIN-protected parent area. Payment is
handled by **Google Play**; we never see card details. To check whether Premium
is active, the app uses **RevenueCat** (our subscription provider), which
receives an anonymous app user ID, the purchase and subscription status, and
basic device and app information needed to validate purchases. RevenueCat's
privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. This information is used
only to provide Premium, never for advertising.

## Permissions

- **Microphone** — only while Aiko is waiting for a spoken answer, and only for
  on-device recognition. A parent can turn voice answers off in the parent area;
  the microphone is then never used.
- **Internet** — only to check the Premium subscription with Google Play and
  RevenueCat. Learning content works offline.

## Children's privacy

The app is designed for children and for families. We do not knowingly collect
personal information from children. Purchases and settings are behind a parent
PIN and a grown-up check.

## Your choices and requests

- Delete on-device data: uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.
- Purchase data held by RevenueCat or Google: contact us and we will request
  deletion on your behalf.

## Changes

If this policy changes we will update the effective date and, for significant
changes, tell parents in the app's parent area.
